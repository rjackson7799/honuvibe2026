SOURCE OF TRUTH — the existing "Hero Voice System" (do not contradict this; codify it)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Hero headlines already use one of TWO voices, chosen by page intent:

• ACTION voice — bold sans, tight tracking, big. For transactional/conversion pages where
  the user is being asked to DO something (Home, Contact, checkout moments).
• EDITORIAL voice — italic serif (DM Serif Display 400), tight leading. For narrative
  "chapter" pages where the user is invited to READ (Learn, Explore, Partnerships, About).

Rules already in force (keep them):
- Pick the voice from page intent, not aesthetics. CTA-first → action. Story-first → editorial.
- Section headings inside a page (h2/h3) always stay upright DM Serif Display 400, regardless
  of which hero voice the page opened with.
- Do NOT invent a third hero voice. If a page fits neither, default to editorial.
- JP equivalents: Action → Noto Sans JP weight 700. Editorial → Noto Serif JP italic if it
  renders well, otherwise Noto Sans JP weight 500 (use a weight contrast, not fake italic).

JP writing rules: line-height 1.7–1.8; letter-spacing 0.02–0.04em; never text-justify;
word-break: break-all acceptable. Never machine-translate — write JP natively.

The Studio sub-brand differs: its action accent color is CORAL (the main brand's teal stays
the education color). Its tone is B2B/agency: "Grow without growing a team," "Proof, not
promises," "We don't just talk about it. We ship it," "Start a project, not a hiring spree."

# HonuVibe Voice Guide

## 1. Voice in one paragraph
The whole brand's voice, distilled. What we always sound like; what we never sound like.

## 2. Profile A — HonuVibe Education voice (Courses + Vault)
- Personality (3–5 adjectives, each with a one-line gloss).
- The two hero voices (Action, Editorial): when to use each, and the JP equivalent.
- Do / Don't table (at least 6 rows) — real phrasings, not abstractions.
- CTA principles: what a HonuVibe education CTA sounds like (verbs, length, promise).
- **Example lines — EN and JP side by side**, at least:
  · 4 Action-voice headlines · 4 Editorial-voice headlines
  · 6 CTA button labels · 3 reassurance/risk-reversal lines · 3 urgency lines
  For each, give EN and the native JP. Mark which hero voice each headline is.

## 3. Profile B — HonuVibe Studio voice (Design services, B2B)
- How it differs from Profile A (audience, register, the coral/"proof-not-promises" identity).
- Do / Don't table (at least 6 rows).
- **Example lines — EN only** (Studio is EN-only for now): 4 headlines, 6 CTA labels,
  3 proof/credibility lines, 2 objection-handling lines.

## 4. Bilingual rules (quick reference)
The JP typography + writing rules above, plus your guidance on tone parity: how to keep JP
feeling native and equal in weight to EN, not like a translation.

## 5. Words we use / words we avoid
A short lexicon: preferred terms, banned clichés (e.g. avoid "unlock your potential,"
"revolutionize," "game-changer"), and how we refer to ourselves.
